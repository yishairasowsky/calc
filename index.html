A tesseract is the 4D analog of a cube — 16 corners, 32 edges, each corner joined to 4 neighbors instead of 3.

It's rotated through two 4D planes (driven by the time control below), then projected 4D → 3D → your screen, the same way a cube's shadow is a 2D projection of a 3D object.

Tip: use x as the variable. Implicit multiplication works: 2x, 2sin(x). Type t for a live, animatable time variable.
